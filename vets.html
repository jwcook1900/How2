Why this exists

Medications, feeding instructions, exercise restrictions, wound care and follow-up advice, all delivered in one emotional moment. It's a lot to remember.

How often do I give this? Can she eat? Is this normal? What if I miss a dose? Reception and nurses answer them all again, constantly.

📱

Built for clinical caution

Your team stays in control.

🩺

The clinic remains the author

The AI changes presentation, not advice. It never invents a dose, a frequency or a warning sign. Unclear details become visible flags, and publishing is gated behind your team confirming them.

🔒

Locked by default

Every recovery guide is encrypted with a simple code your front desk writes on the paperwork. We can't read it, and neither can anyone without the code.

🏥

Your clinic on every guide

Save your logo and contact details once. Every new guide starts with your branding, your phone number and your after-hours line, automatically.

🇦🇺

Stored in Australia

Encrypted in transit and at rest on AWS in Sydney. No AI training on your data, no advertising, deleted on request. The full detail is public, in plain English.

Security & privacy

We can't read your discharge notes.

Your discharge notes are encrypted on your own computer before they reach us, with a code only your clinic sets. We can't read them. Neither can anyone who gets hold of the link, because the code that opens it never travels with the link. It goes home on the paperwork.

  • No readable copy sitting in an inbox
  • No wrong address moment you can't take back
  • Nothing a search engine can ever find

One thing worth knowing: to turn your notes into a guide, they pass through our AI provider in the United States. Transiently, never stored there, never used to train anything. Everything else stays on AWS in Sydney.
