Our story

Why we built GotIt Guides

Hi, I'm James. I live in Sydney with my girlfriend Jude, our golden retriever Percy, and my dog Whiskey.

At two years old, Whiskey was diagnosed with idiopathic epilepsy. We quickly learned there's no silver bullet. Managing it means multiple medications, up to three times a day. It was a life-changing moment.

It also meant that every time I went away, whoever looked after him needed to understand exactly what to do: the medications, the timing, the signs to watch for, and who to call if something went wrong.

So I'd write pages and pages of instructions. It worked, but a wall of text is the last thing anyone wants to scroll through in a stressful moment.

GotIt Guides came out of those pages: one clear, living guide a sitter can actually follow, with routines, medication, behaviour notes and emergency contacts, in a single link you share once and update anytime.

If it helps one more pet get looked after properly, it's done its job.

A little less stress before you go away.

Set it up once before your next trip, sitter booking or guest stay. No rewriting the same instructions every time, and a guide that's clear enough for someone to follow in the moment.
