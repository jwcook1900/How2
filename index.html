Why it's better

Confidence for them. Peace of mind for you.

A text thread gets lost and a document goes out of date. A guide is built for the person stepping in, so they can act without guessing.

✅

Nothing important gets missed

Prompts and structured sections help you capture the details you'd otherwise forget until you're already away.

💡

They know what's normal

What's completely normal for them, but might worry someone else? A dog that eats lying down. A hot-water system that clanks. Write it down and nobody panics.

🎥

Show, don't just explain

Photos and short videos make the fiddly things easier to follow than any written description.

🔄

Update once, share the latest

Routines change. Edit the guide and everyone with the link sees the current version, no re-sending documents.

🗂️

Everything has its place

Routines, contacts, instructions, access details and emergency information stay easy to find in the moment.

📝

You stay in the loop

Sitters, carers and guests can log what's done: medications given, walks taken, anything worth recording.

Trust & privacy

Yours to control.

Make it as simple or detailed as you like, and decide exactly who can open it.

🔗

Unlisted by default

Guides are unlisted. Anyone with the link can open them unless you add a guide code.

🔒

Lock anything sensitive

Add a guide code and the contents are encrypted on your device before they're stored. Even we can't read them.

✏️

Change your mind anytime

Update the guide and the same link stays current. Remove it from your dashboard, or email us and we'll delete it.

Our story

Why we built GotIt Guides

Hi, I'm James. I live in Sydney with my girlfriend Jude, our golden retriever Percy, and my dog Whiskey.

At two years old, Whiskey was diagnosed with idiopathic epilepsy. We quickly learned there's no silver bullet. Managing it means multiple medications, up to three times a day. It was a life-changing moment.

It also meant that every time I went away, whoever looked after him needed to understand exactly what to do: the medications, the timing, the signs to watch for, and who to call if something went wrong.

So I'd write pages and pages of instructions. It worked, but a wall of text is the last thing anyone wants to scroll through in a stressful moment.

GotIt Guides came out of those pages: one clear, living guide a sitter can actually follow, with routines, medication, behaviour notes and emergency contacts, in a single link you share once and update anytime.

If it helps one more pet get looked after properly, it's done its job.

A little less to worry about when you hand over.

Set it up once before the next trip, sitter booking, guest stay or week of helping out. No rewriting the same instructions every time, and a guide clear enough to follow in the moment.
