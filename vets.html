Why this exists

Owners are overwhelmed at discharge.

"People don't listen to me. They're distracted." A vet, at the end of a discharge consult. This product exists because of that sentence.
😵‍💫

Too much, too fast

After surgery, owners are anxious and flooded with information. Verbal instructions, printed sheets and medication advice mostly don't stick.

📞

The same calls, every day

How often do I give this? Is that behaviour normal? Can she eat yet? Should the wound look like this? Reception and nurses answer them all again, daily.

📄

Printed sheets don't get read

Long blocks of text get skimmed at best. A clean, friendly guide on the owner's phone actually gets opened, and stays with them through recovery.

How it works

Your notes in, an owner-ready guide out.

1

Upload

The discharge notes you already write: a PDF, photos of the paperwork, or the emailed summary.

2

Review

Every instruction is copied exactly, never invented. Anything unclear is flagged for your team to confirm before publishing.

3

Send

One link by SMS, email or WhatsApp, locked with a code you write on the paperwork. Dose reminders land in the owner's calendar with one tap.

Built for clinical caution

Your team stays in control.

🩺

The clinic remains the author

The AI changes presentation, not advice. It never invents a dose, a frequency or a warning sign. Unclear details become visible flags, and publishing is gated behind your team confirming them.

🔒

Locked by default

Every discharge guide is encrypted with a simple code your front desk writes on the paperwork. We can't read it, and neither can anyone without the code.

🏥

Your clinic on every guide

Save your logo and contact details once. Every new discharge guide starts with your branding, your phone number and your after-hours line, automatically.

🇦🇺

Stored in Australia

Encrypted in transit and at rest on AWS in Sydney. No AI training on your data, no advertising, deleted on request. The full detail is public, in plain English.
