Who's behind this

Hi, I'm James Cook.

I live in Sydney, Australia with my girlfriend Jude, our golden retriever Percy, and my dog Whiskey — a cocker spaniel with idiopathic epilepsy. Managing his condition means multiple medications a day, and every time we go away, whoever looks after him needs to know exactly what to do.

Privacy, in plain English

Your guides are yours.

🔗

Private by default

Guides aren't listed anywhere public. The only people who can open one are the people you share its link with.

🔒

Lock anything sensitive

Add a guide code and the contents are encrypted on your device before they're stored — we couldn't read them if we tried. Only the guide's name stays visible, so the right person knows what they've received.

🙈

No ads, no trackers

No ad networks, no tracking pixels, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing. Our only analytics is an anonymous counter — which pages get viewed and roughly where visitors arrive from — never who you are.

✉️

We only know what you tell us

You don't need an account to create or view a guide. We only hold your email if you give it to us — to send you your guide links, sign in with Google, or join the updates list.

🇦🇺

Stored in Australia

Everything is hosted on Amazon Web Services in Sydney.

🗑️

Delete anytime

Remove a guide from your dashboard, or email us the link and we'll delete it — no questions asked.
