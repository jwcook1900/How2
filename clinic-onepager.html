Digital Recovery Guides owners actually follow.

Your clinic already writes the discharge notes. We turn them into a guide the owner reads on their phone, reviewed by your team before it goes anywhere. No rewriting, no new system, no app for owners to download.

How it fits your day

  1. Write the discharge notes exactly as you do now, in the system you already use.
  2. Upload the PDF, a photo or the text. Front desk drops in whatever exists.
  3. The guide drafts itself. Every instruction copied exactly. Anything unclear is flagged, never guessed.
  4. Your team reviews. Confirm medications, warning signs and follow-up before publishing.
  5. Send one link by SMS, email or WhatsApp, locked with a code you write on the paperwork.
  6. The owner recovers with it, dose reminders and all, one tap from their calendar.

Why clinics ask for it

  • Owners leave overwhelmed. Medications, feeding, restrictions, wound care and follow-up, all in one emotional moment.
  • The same questions, every day. How often do I give this? Can she eat? Is this normal? Reception answers them all again.
  • Paper gets skimmed once and lost. A guide stays on the phone for the whole recovery.

Your team stays the author

The AI changes presentation, not advice. It never invents a dose, a frequency or a warning sign. Unclear details become visible flags your team actions before publishing, and the veterinary-advice disclaimer is on every guide.

We can't read your discharge notes.

Your discharge notes are encrypted on your own computer before they reach us, with a code only your clinic sets. We can't read them. Neither can anyone who gets hold of the link, because the code that opens it never travels with the link. It goes home on the paperwork.

  • No readable copy sitting in an inbox
  • No wrong address moment you can't take back
  • Nothing a search engine can ever find
  • Stored on AWS in Sydney, encrypted in transit and at rest
  • No AI training on your data, no advertising, deleted on request
  • Consent confirmed by your team before anything is published

One thing worth knowing: to turn your notes into a guide, they pass through our AI provider in the United States. Transiently, never stored there, never used to train anything. Everything else stays in Sydney. Full detail at gotitguides.com/clinic-security.html and the data processing summary beside it.
